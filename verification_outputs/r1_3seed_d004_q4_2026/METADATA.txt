Root cause (diagnostic, not a recorded metric)
----------------------------------------------
The framework's build_initial_state -> _synthesize_latent_like_tensor draws a
near-uniform simplex (1/K + U(0, 1e-6)) for every record. Under the (now live)
deterministic real flow this collapses every record onto ONE trajectory:

  seed42 framework: 121 unique sequences / 1000, and exactly 1 unique 30-AA prefix
  seed42 baseline : 1000 unique sequences / 1000

Diversity came only from per-record length truncation (121 distinct lengths).
The pre-D-004 runs escaped the collapse because the zero velocity field left the
restart-blend perturbation RNG as the sole source of per-record diversity.

Bounded probe (N=20, diagnostic only): monkeypatching the init to a per-record
random simplex softmax(randn(L, K)) — the Wave-41 tool's noise convention —
restores full diversity (20/20 unique sequences, 20/20 unique 30-AA prefixes) and
protein-like output; HMMER at N=20 gives baseline 4 vs framework 3 (inconclusive
at this n). This motivates follow-up D-004b (per-record stochastic init for the
real-classifier path); it is NOT applied here.
