Reading
-------
On the genuine real-forward basis with a per-record stochastic restart init, R1
is a direction-consistent, Bonferroni-significant framework WIN on all 3 seeds
(mean +105 hits, +66% on the seed-42 baseline). This is the OPPOSITE of the
D-004 zero-collapse reading (d_pooled = -0.4013) and is ABOVE the pre-D-004
contaminated run (d_pooled = +0.110175). The pooled effect (0.156) still sits
below the 0.8 STRONG-promotion bar, so R1 remains a small-effect PARTIAL-class
cell; what changed is that its real-forward basis is now a WIN, not a collapse.

Caveat (must be carried with this result)
-----------------------------------------
The D-004b restart init is a TOOL-CONVENTION surrogate (softmax(randn) simplex),
not the upstream intervention/mutation sampler. A reviewer may ask whether the
gain is driven by the framework's restart-blend or by the random init alone;
disentangling that (init-only vs full framework arm) is the natural next control.
