## 3. Interpretation (why this closes G31)

The 11-cell verdict distribution is the framework's primary empirical claim (the systematic empirical evidence of §7.1) and is restated across §7.1 / §7.2 / §11.1 / §11.2 in two flavours: an **aggregate** form (`4 STRONG + 1 TIE + 6 PARTIAL/SUPP = 11`, the reviewer-friendly headline) and a **disaggregated** form (`4 STRONG + 1 TIE + 2 PARTIAL/single-seed + 4 SUPP-only = 11`, the load-bearing reading). The disaggregation is load-bearing because:

1. **Honest disclosure**: the disaggregation separates the 2 PARTIAL/single-seed cells (R1 LineageFlow HMMER + R3 FlowMol3 fg_dev, both honestly disclosed underpowered single-seed readings) from the 4 SUPP-only K-cells (K1 FlowMol3 RDKit, K4 LineageFlow hmmscan_total_hits (Pfam-A coverage), K5 LineageFlow hmmscan_total_hits delta, K7 LineageFlow novelty). These two sub-categories have qualitatively different evidentiary status — the 2 PARTIAL/single-seed cells are demoted under the status-promotion bar but cleared Bonferroni-significance, while the 4 SUPP-only K-cells are demoted to supplementary after the multi-pass audit surfaced per-cell granularity inconsistencies — and conflating them under a single "PARTIAL/SUPP" label risks erasing that distinction at first reading.
2. **Reviewer-facing transparency**: the disaggregation matches the §7.2 per-cell table (where each cell's status is enumerated individually) and the §11.1 honest-verdict paragraph (which enumerates the same 11 cells by name), so a reviewer who reads the per-cell table and then looks at the headline sees the same disaggregated numbers — no surprise at the aggregate layer.
3. **TNNLS Q1 reviewer expectation**: at a Q1 venue the reviewer reads the per-cell section first (the §7.2 row-by-row narrative) and then skims the conclusion (the §11.1 honest-verdict paragraph). Surfacing the disaggregation at the top of §7.2 — before any per-cell narrative — ensures the disaggregation is the FIRST encounter the reviewer has with the 11-cell distribution, and the aggregate is the closing reference.

The canonical statement's wording is intentionally explicit: *"The disaggregation is the load-bearing reading; the aggregate is the reviewer-friendly headline."* This sentence makes the relationship between the two flavours explicit, so that no reviewer can mistake the aggregate for the load-bearing claim or the disaggregation for an aside.
