## 3. Interpretation (why this closes G20)

The gap (G20) was that the paper §6 uses Lemmas 1-3 + Theorems 4-5 — three framework property lemmas (composition closure, byte-determinism, tamper-evidence) and two convergence rate theorem bounds (multi-round re-inference convergence, R5b restart-blend attenuation) — while the supplementary §S1 uses Lemmas 2-5 — paper Theorem 1 derivation lemmas (the four-lemma derivation chain that anchors the convergence theory in the supplementary). The two lemma sequences overlap at Lemmas 2-3 numerically (Lemma 2 in §6 = state-machine byte-determinism; Lemma 2 in §S1 = paper Theorem 1 derivation lemma; Lemma 3 in §6 = ledger tamper-evidence; Lemma 3 in §S1 = paper Theorem 1 derivation lemma), but they semantically name entirely different statements. Without an explicit reconciliation note, a reviewer reading §6 would see "Lemma 2 — State-machine byte-determinism" and might search the supplementary for the same statement under the same number, only to find a different lemma entirely.

The reconciliation paragraph in the §6 preamble closes G20 by:

1. **Naming the two conventions explicitly**: §6 Lemmas 1-3 = framework properties; §S1 Lemmas 2-5 = paper Theorem 1 derivation lemmas. The numerical overlap (Lemmas 2-3 appear in both) is acknowledged but immaterial to the cross-reference resolution.
2. **Naming Theorems 4-5 = convergence rate bounds**: this anchors that the §6 convention extends Lemmas 1-3 (three properties) by adding Theorems 4-5 (two rate bounds) — a total of 5 numbered items in §6 — while §S1 has only Lemmas 2-5 (four derivation lemmas). The numerical convention difference makes any "Lemma 4" reference resolve to the §S1 derivation chain, not to a §6 theorem (because §6 has no Lemma 4).
3. **Specifying the resolution rule**: "cross-references between paper §6 and supplementary §S1 are resolved by content, not by number". This explicitly tells the reviewer that when §6 says "Lemma 2" they should match by content to the §6 §6.3 "State-machine byte-determinism" body, NOT to §S1's "Lemma 2" derivation lemma — and vice versa.
4. **Positioning in the §6 preamble**: the note is placed at the §6.1 → §6.2 boundary, so that immediately AFTER the §6.1 preamble (which names the three properties + the §6.5 Theorem 4 reference) and BEFORE the §6.2 Lemma 1 body, the reviewer encounters the cross-reference clarification. This is the optimal location because §6.1 already introduces the §6 numbering convention; adding the §S1 cross-reference note at the same boundary is the natural extension and avoids introducing a footnote or appendix-level pointer that the reviewer would have to discover separately.

The reconciliation paragraph's wording is intentionally explicit: *"The two lemma sequences are distinct; cross-references between paper §6 and supplementary §S1 are resolved by content, not by number."* This sentence makes the rule explicit at the §6 preamble, so that no reviewer can mistake the numerical overlap for semantic co-incidence.
