## Composition / interaction notes

* `CodimensionSheetScheduler` is the **paper-grounded** implementation of paper Lemma 2 + Lemma 3 as a first-class `SchedulerProtocol` member (CLM-006, registered in `SCHEDULER_REGISTRY["codimension_sheet"]`).
* `EvidenceDrivenScheduler` is **the loop-closing scheduler**: it consumes per-round metrics via `record_round_feedback` (registered under `SCHEDULER_REGISTRY["evidence_driven"]`); it amplifies the PID-lite signal above the `round(n_cap × N)` rounding threshold (CLM-032, fix-v2 plan §4).
* `BoundedMergeOperator` is the **paper-grounded merge envelope**: it enforces Lemma 4's `e_ρ/4` floor as the structural noise-floor guarantee (CLM-020, CLM-025).
* All three algorithms are **inference-only** — they consume the user's pre-trained model without modifying it.
* All three are **byte-stable**: their `.to_config()` / `.from_config()` round-trips preserve the configuration; the merge envelope is fail-closed rather than silent-swap.
