# FlowA Paper Evidence

Translate verified results into publication evidence.

- Read `docs/CLAIMS.md`, `docs/CONSOLIDATED_RESULTS.md`, `docs/INSIGHTS.md`, and the relevant audit before editing prose.
- Every quantitative statement must point to a result artifact or audit with model, metric, N, seed policy, environment, and comparison direction.
- Distinguish SUPPORTED, NOT_SIGNIFICANT, UNDERPOWERED, REGRESSES, and DEFERRED; do not promote provisional results by wording alone.
- Update linked surfaces together when needed and run claims/docs consistency checks.
- Preserve caveats such as metric-definition gaps, CPU/GPU differences, and pending user-gated sweeps.
